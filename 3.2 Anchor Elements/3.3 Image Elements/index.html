Matthew's top 5 Favourite Movies

  • Goodfellas

    This film is a staple for me. Its narrative approach is something I love in film, if executed properly. Martin Scorcese is and always will be my favorite film maker. It was hard not to put more than two movies of his on this list.

  • The Godfather

    Despite me not seeing this until my late 20s, I can honestly say I've never seen anything like this masterpiece. "Goodfellas" only tops it because I'm partial to a Scorcese/De Niro team up. Speaking of De Niro, that why Part II of the Godfather made the list.

  • There Will Be Blood

    This movie is a work of art and really made me question the oil industry and all its pursuit of wealth has to deal with. Not to mention Daniel Day Lewis being one hell of a prescense on screen. He also falls within my top five favorite actors.

  • The Godfather Part II

    This piece is the best sequel to grace the planet. I love the way it steps into the past to to exemplify the differences between the rises of power across generations. I also must add, De Niro playing a young Vito Corleone is a chef's kiss.

  • The Departed

    Some regard this as Scorcese's worst film. I beg to differ. I like this film because there is so much hidden within the dialogue, there's something new to figure out with each watch.
