About Me

Hi, I'm Matthew Edmonds— a Writer and Web Developer, based in Chicago, IL. With a passion for film and acting, I specialize in script writing and project development. Whether I'm working on my most recent film project " Chickentown " or exploring new ideas, I love turning creativity into results. Over the years, I’ve gained experience in writing, directing, and acting, through partnerships with [Good Object], DharMann Studios, and the list goes on; constantly striving to learn and grow. When I’m not working, you can't find me.

By day, I try to build websites that don’t suck, write stories that stick, and hope to design things that make people feel something. By night, I’m probably sketching new ideas, chasing down inspiration, or getting lost in a good book or a weird side project.

I believe in the magic of bold ideas, thoughtful design, and making things that matter — not just look pretty. My favorite projects usually start with a "what if?" and end with a quiet "hell yes."

Some things I’m into: 🎨 Turning blank pages into something beautiful ⚡ Making tech feel human 🌍 Collaborating across cultures, time zones, and playlists 🧠 Learning new things just because Let’s make something awesome together. Or just talk about weird art, creative burnout, or your favorite kind of pizza. I’m down either way.
